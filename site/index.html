Questions

Is there a phone app?

Android is coming soon to Google Play, with the same full-screen red alerts, and iPhone after that. Today, Ignore Me Not runs on Windows 10 and 11 and on Macs with macOS 11 or later.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC." Is it safe?

Windows shows this for apps from small, independent makers. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Ignore Me Not doesn't need admin rights and keeps everything on your computer.

How do I install it?

On Windows, open IgnoreMeNot-Setup.exe, click Next, then Finish. On a Mac, open the downloaded file, drag Ignore Me Not onto the Applications folder, then open it from Applications. A test alert appears, then the app opens so you can add your first reminder. On a Mac it also lives in your menu bar, at the top right of your screen.

My Mac says it "could not verify" Ignore Me Not. Is it safe?

Macs show this for apps from small, independent makers that aren't registered with Apple yet. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then enter your password. You only need to do this once. Ignore Me Not keeps everything on your computer.

Does my computer need to be on?

Yes. Alerts show while your computer is on and awake. Ignore Me Not starts by itself when you turn your computer on.

Do I need internet?

No for the Free plan. Premium checks your subscription once a day and keeps working for up to 14 days offline.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

Premium works until your paid period ends. After that, your first 5 reminders keep working and the rest are paused, not deleted. Subscribe again anytime to bring them back.
