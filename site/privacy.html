Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

The short version: your reminders stay on your computer. We don't have accounts, we don't track you, and we don't sell data.

This policy explains what information Ignore Me Not handles and why. The maker of Ignore Me Not ("we", "us") is responsible for the information described in this policy.

1. Information that stays on your computer

Your reminders, notes, settings and calendar links are saved only on your own computer, in the app's folder. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

2. Information we do receive

  • Purchases: when you buy Premium, our reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com, processes your order and payment. We receive your email address, purchase details and license key, but never your full card details.
  • License check: if you have Premium, the app sends your license key and your computer's name to our license server at ignoremenot.app about once a day to confirm your subscription is active. Nothing else is sent.
  • Emails you send us: if you contact us, we keep your message and email address to reply.

3. Calendar alerts

If you add a calendar link, the app downloads your calendar directly from Google, Microsoft or Apple to show alerts. Your calendar data goes straight to your computer and never passes through us.

4. This website

We don't use cookies, analytics or ads on this website. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

5. How we use your information

Only to deliver Premium, answer your messages, handle refunds, and meet legal or tax obligations. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

We keep purchase records as long as the law requires for tax and accounting, and support emails for up to 2 years.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how it's used. Email hello@ignoremenot.app and we'll reply within 15 days.

8. Children

Ignore Me Not isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Privacy questions? Email hello@ignoremenot.app.
